# ULTRON Phase 9 MLOps Lifecycle

**Recommended gate: GO TO PHASE 10 REAL TELEMETRY VALIDATION**, for separately approved research only. Phase 9 versions and evaluates the approved analytical suite offline. It does not activate shadow inference, deploy a policy, deliver notifications, or replace a trained model.

## Architecture and scope

The governance package is not imported by the Windows collector, SQLite persistence, Next.js, or the Python inference service. It reads a bounded registry, validates trusted local artifacts, and generates offline reports. Journals are separate files, not production SQLite tables. Shadow lifecycle observability is the offline evaluation and transition audit; the existing read-only dashboard remains unchanged and does not indicate that an experimental policy is running. Registry, evaluation, or drift failures have no runtime control path. Phase 8-D cooperative writer admission and the deterministic critical rules are unmodified.

The registry describes seven versioned components: reduced hybrid detection, process persistence forecasting, feature settings, telemetry, inference API, hypothetical eligibility, and finding identity plus lifecycle. CPU/workload, disk capacity, and process responsiveness are the detection scope. Only process responsiveness forecasts. The fixed policy remains `persistence:1800:0.65` with a 240-second minimum lead. Eligibility stays at `phase8-offline-v1`. There is no new model, training, model server, or MLflow dependency; the local JSON registry and append-only evidence suffice for a single-device offline workflow.

## Manifest integrity and policy lifecycle

`policy_registry.json` contains an approved baseline description (1.0.0) and an experimental metadata-only candidate (1.0.1). Each record has a strict schema, approved scope, versioned component IDs, evaluation reference, dependency versions, analytical fingerprints, and SHA-256 hashes for the bundled configuration, selected engine and contract sources, Node ownership and eligibility code, and Phase 8-D gate evidence. A pinned analytical identity excludes only governance metadata. Artifact names map to fixed source paths; manifests cannot specify executable code, arbitrary paths, or commands. Exact bytes are checked locally, and the existing Phase 6 loader verifies its canonical bundle and runtime feature fingerprints. Exact dependency mismatches fail closed. The pin protects against modified manifests and artifacts relative to this reviewed code; it is not a signed software supply-chain system. An independently modified checkout is outside that trust boundary.

Initial states are experimental or previously approved for shadow. Validation requires all six evaluation gates plus integrity, schema, fingerprints, dependency, API and scope compatibility. The evaluation record binds the manifest, inputs, configuration and evidence by hash. A candidate moves to validated offline only on PASS; failed and inconclusive evaluations retain the baseline. Even a PASS cannot simulate promotion without explicit approval. Approval in the fixture does not change the running service.

Rollback simulations for invalid findings, exceeded limits, unavailability and incompatibility reverify the approved baseline and mark the candidate as a rollback candidate. When the baseline cannot be verified there is no selected simulated policy and the audit reports baseline unavailable; it does not claim a silent fallback. A rejected or rolled-back candidate may be retired in the offline simulator. The evaluation journal writes a staged, flushed record before atomically publishing its name on the same local filesystem; duplicate names cannot overwrite previous decisions. The fixture exports PASS, FAIL and INCONCLUSIVE records separately. It does not promise recovery in the event of storage media failure.

## Data quality and drift

The evaluator uses the existing sanitized telemetry contract and feature gap/cache semantics. It accepts at most 1,800 rows and 12 hours. Missing, stale, cached and fresh observations are distinct. Distribution summaries require at least 30 independent fresh observations, 50% fresh coverage and one hour of continuity in the latest segment. Cached deep observations are counted for quality but do not amplify distribution evidence; expired cached data is marked stale. Gaps, irregular cadence, unsupported measurements, unavailable sensors, and short warmup are reported separately. Incompatible schema and incompatible reference are distinct from insufficient evidence and from no drift detected.

The reference, development and locked evaluation each have a different deterministic seed and time range. These are new synthetic Phase 9 inputs, with per-input hashes and no historical holdout reuse. Stable, constant, and sparse cases showed no drift; the engineered CPU, cadence and power-context shifts were detected; missing, mostly cached, and short histories were insufficient. Continuous features use robust median and interquartile-range comparisons with absolute effect floors. Sparse counts use nonzero prevalence, not division by near-zero scale. Missingness, cadence and power-context mix are informational. The reports do not establish a calibrated real-device operating point, prove a healthy device, or trigger retraining, rollback, alerts or remediation.
